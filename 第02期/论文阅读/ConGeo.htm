      “existing learning pipelines are orientationspecific or FoV-specific, demanding separate model training for different ground view variations” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 1) 现有方法存在的局限性

      “Northaligned spatial correspondence and predefined FoVs in the training data” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 1)

      “by enforcing proximity between ground view variations of the same location” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 1) 提高鲁棒性和抵抗力的方法

      “cyclically shifting a ground view panorama by a random angle to obtain orientation variations, or further reducing the FoV from 360◦ to 70◦, 90◦, or 180◦ to simulate limited ground view information” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 2) 通过位移移动地图（改变原本的北对齐）或者缩减视角提高数据集的鲁棒性

      “they train and evaluate models separately for each setting” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 2) 只对特定的方向或者角度进行训练限制了泛化能力

      “the models are often biased toward training data to capture specific spatial correspondence” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 2) 模型训练的局限性从而限制了泛化，怎样设计突破这个局限性，实现更好的泛化效果？

      “a robust model should capture consistent features for the same location regardless of orientations or FoVs and identify the same reference image across ground view variations” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 2) 提取特征的底层原理

      “its augmented counterpart” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 3) 增强版本是航拍分支，对同一张卫星参考图做两次独立的随机增强（论文中是随机旋转）

      “mitigating potential shortcuts found in the training data” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 3) 与上一段落提到的shorcut带来的负面影像对应，这里减少

      “using a model-agnostic learning pipeline” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 3) 通用学习方法，不拘泥于单个模型

      “the trade-off between the model’s focus on geometric or semantic cues” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 3) 对于几何和语义的选择会影响变化检测

      “The standard approach uses a siamese network [2] to encode the ground view and the aerial images and optimizes a well-designed loss to align the feature embeddings.” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 3)

      “First, specific FoV information is required for aerial image/feature cropping during both training and testing, while in real-world data this strong prior is unknown, especially during testing. Second, models trained on a specific setting cannot generalize and do not perform satisfactorily on all FoV scenarios. Thus several specialised models are trained for each setting.” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 4) 对于未知方向和有限视角现有方法存在的局限性

      “aims to enhance the robustness of geo-localization models by enforcing proximity between ground view variations and their original representations” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 4)

      “can be cyclically shifted with a random angle and restricted by a specific FoV (e.g., 70◦, 90◦, 180◦, and 360◦)” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 5) 地面图像的增强版本的由来

      “A random orientation angle θ is first used to horizontally shift the ground image, followed by the application of a FoV angle α to crop the panorama query to a FoV query Iq∗ = Tq(Iq|θ, α).” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 5) 增强图像的具体操作

      “produce similar representations between the original images (North-aligned, full panorama) and their transformed counterpart” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 5) 单视图对比学习的目的

      “enforce the similarity between two distorted versions of the same reference image using random data augmentations, aiming to obtain more robust feature representations” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 6) 航空对比损失与地面对比损失的区别，航空图像是两个是扰乱图像相似

      “vanilla loss” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 6) 原始损失：强调「未经修改、来自基模型」，与 ConGeo 新增的三项对比损失构成对照

      “Combining all the terms previously described” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 6) 集合所有损失，最终完成对比学习目标

      “the orientation angle θ is randomly drawn between 0◦ and 360◦ while the FoV angle α is set to 180◦” (Mi 等, 2024, p. 7) θ 管的是「朝哪个方向看」，做的是循环平移，不丢任何信息；α 管的是「一眼能看多宽」，做的是裁剪，会真的丢掉一半视野
